Got Questions?

Everything you need to know.

How long does the battery really last?

Mochi provides up to 30 hours of continuous music and calls on a single charge. If you're in a rush before class, a rapid 10-minute USB-C boost delivers over 4 hours of listening time.

Will Mochi fit comfortably on smaller heads?

Yes, absolutely! We designed the ergonomic headband arc with calibrated gentle clamp force and a featherlight 250-gram chassis, ensuring it stays secure without sliding down or causing temple pain.

What is the warranty and return policy?

Every Mochi headphone comes with our 1-Year Comprehensive Replacement Guarantee. If you ever change your mind about your colour choice, we offer a 14-day hassle-free doorstep exchange policy.

How clear is the microphone for study calls & Zoom?

Mochi features dual environmental-noise-cancelling beamforming microphones that isolate your voice while suppressing background ambient noise from cafes or roommates.

Can I connect to both my laptop and phone at once?

Yes, Mochi supports seamless multipoint Bluetooth 5.3 pairing. You can watch lectures on your laptop and instantly take an incoming call on your phone without re-pairing.
